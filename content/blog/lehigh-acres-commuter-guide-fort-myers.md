## Drive times (realistic ranges)

- Downtown Fort Myers: 25–40 minutes depending on rush hour.
- I-75 access (Gateway Blvd): ~15–20 minutes.
- Southwest Florida International Airport (RSW): 25–35 minutes.
- Gulf Coast Town Center: 25–35 minutes.

Tip: Leave 15 minutes earlier on peak school days; eastbound morning traffic stays lighter.
