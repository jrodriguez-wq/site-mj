# How to Buy a New Construction Home in Florida

Buying directly from a builder is different from resale. Follow these steps when you purchase a **new construction home for sale** in Southwest Florida.

## Step 1: Define budget and financing

Get pre-approved for a mortgage or confirm cash funds. Compare monthly payment to Rent to Own if you need flexibility — see [financing options](/home-buying-guide).

## Step 2: Choose community and model

M.J. Newell builds in **LaBelle** and **Lehigh Acres**. Browse [homes for sale](/new-homes-for-sale) and [floor plans](/models).

## Step 3: Visit and compare

Schedule a tour. Review standard features, upgrades, lot size, and total price.

## Step 4: Contract and deposit

Sign the purchase agreement. Understand build timeline, warranties, and closing costs.

## Step 5: Construction and walkthrough

Stay in touch during build. Do a final walkthrough before closing.

## Step 6: Closing and move-in

Close with your lender or cash. Receive keys and warranty documents.
