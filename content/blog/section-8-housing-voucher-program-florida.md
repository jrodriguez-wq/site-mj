## What is Section 8?

The Housing Choice Vouchers Program, also known as Section 8, is a federal assistance program that provides rental assistance to eligible low- and moderate-income families. However, many people don't realize that Section 8 vouchers can also be used to help purchase a home in certain circumstances.

### How Section 8 Works

Section 8 works as a rental subsidy that allows families to pay a reasonable amount of their income toward rent. Generally, families will pay no more than 40 percent of their adjusted monthly income toward their rent share, and the rental subsidy covers the rest.

**Key Features of Section 8:**
- Provides rental assistance to eligible families
- Allows families to choose where they want to live
- Can be used in every major market in the United States
- Administered locally by public housing agencies (PHAs)
- Helps families afford quality housing

## Section 8 and Homeownership

While Section 8 is primarily known as a rental assistance program, it can also be used to help families become homeowners through special homeownership programs offered by local Public Housing Agencies (PHAs).

### Using Section 8 for Homeownership

Some PHAs offer homeownership programs that allow Section 8 voucher holders to use their vouchers to help pay for mortgage payments instead of rent. This can make homeownership more accessible for families who qualify.

**Benefits of Using Section 8 for Homeownership:**
- Use your voucher to help with mortgage payments
- Build equity in your own home
- Create generational wealth
- Lock in your housing costs
- Invest in your future

## How to Apply for Section 8

If you don't currently have a Section 8 voucher but believe you may be eligible, here's how to apply:

### Step 1: Create a Free Account

Create a free account on [AffordableHousing.com](https://www.affordablehousing.com) to search for open waiting lists in your area.

### Step 2: Search for Open Waiting Lists

Use AffordableHousing.com to search for open Section 8 waiting lists in your area. The website makes it easy to find and apply to multiple waiting lists.

### Step 3: Apply Online

Many waiting lists operated through AffordableHousing.com allow you to apply directly online. For other waiting lists, you may need to contact the housing provider directly.

### Step 4: Wait for Approval

Once you apply, you'll be placed on a waiting list. Wait times vary depending on local market conditions and the number of applicants.

## Who is Eligible for Section 8?

Eligibility for a Housing Choice Voucher is determined by local Public Housing Agencies (PHAs) based on:

### Income Requirements

- **General Rule**: Your income may not exceed 50 percent of the median income for your county or metropolitan area
- **Priority**: 75 percent of vouchers must go to applicants whose incomes don't exceed 30 percent of the median income
- **Contact Your PHA**: Your local PHA can provide specific income limits for your area and family size

### Other Requirements

- Must be at least 18 years old
- Must be a U.S. citizen or have eligible immigration status
- Single persons are eligible, as well as households with or without children
- Must meet other criteria set by your local PHA
