# Rent-to-Own for First-Time Homebuyers

**Never bought a home before? Rent-to-own removes the risk.**

Traditional mortgage is a leap of faith. Rent-to-own is a bridge.

## Why Rent-to-Own is Perfect for First-Time Buyers

### Problem 1: You Don't Have a Down Payment
**Traditional**: Save 5-20% ($10-40k) before you can even start.  
**Rent-to-Own**: Move in with $0 down. Build equity as you live.

### Problem 2: You Don't Know if You're Ready
**Traditional**: Sign a 30-year mortgage immediately.  
**Rent-to-Own**: Test-drive homeownership for 24 months. Then decide.

### Problem 3: Your Credit Isn't Perfect
**Traditional**: Rejected or charged 8%+ interest.  
**Rent-to-Own**: Approved at 520+. Build credit while you live.

### Problem 4: You Don't Understand the Process
**Traditional**: Confusing paperwork, unknown costs, fast timelines.  
**Rent-to-Own**: MJ walks you through every step. 24/7 advisor.

---

## How It Works for First-Timers

### Phase 1: Rent (24 Months)
- Live in your home (it's yours to test)
- Pay market-rate rent
- 15% of rent = down payment credit
- Build credit & savings

### Phase 2: Own (Month 25)
- Apply for mortgage
- MJ advisor helps you navigate approval
- Rent credits apply to down payment
- Sign closing documents
- Become the owner

## What First-Timers Are Worried About

**"What if I don't qualify for a mortgage?"**  
MJ gives you 24 months to improve credit and save. 95% of lease-holders qualify.

**"What if I want to move after 1 year?"**  
You can. You forfeit remaining rent credits. But most stay.

**"What if I hate the home?"**  
24-month lease is long enough to know. Most people love it by then.

**"What if the home has problems?"**  
You get a full inspection before signing. MJ repairs are covered.
