# Rent-to-Own Timeline: From Application to Homeownership

**How long does rent-to-own really take?**

Not as long as you'd think. MJ's process is fast and transparent.

## Day-by-Day Timeline: 12-Month Express Path

### Week 1: Application
- **Day 1**: Online application (5 minutes)
- **Day 2**: Phone interview (20 minutes)
- **Day 3-4**: Credit check, employment verification
- **Day 5**: Approval decision
- **Day 7**: Lease signed, funds deposited

### Months 2-12: Rent & Build
- Pay rent monthly (earns 20% credit)
- Make on-time payments (builds credit history)
- Contact lender at month 9 for pre-approval

### Month 10: Pre-Approval
- Lender reviews rent payment history
- Mortgage pre-approval issued
- Appraisal scheduled

### Month 11: Closing Prep
- Home inspection
- Final walkthrough
- Closing documents prepared

### Month 12: Closing
- Sign documents
- Rent credits applied to down payment
- Keys transferred
- **You own the home**

**Total: 1 year from application to homeownership**

---

## Qualification Requirements

### Income Verification
✓ W-2 employment (most common)  
✓ Self-employed (2 years tax returns)  
✓ Disability/Social Security  
✓ Pension or retirement income  

**Minimum**: Income sufficient to afford 1.2x the monthly rent.

### Employment Stability
✓ 12+ months at current employer (preferred)  
✓ 6+ months acceptable (with gap explanation)  
✓ Career change acceptable if income stable  

### Credit History
✓ 520+ credit score (all three bureaus)  
✓ No active collections (settled acceptable)  
✓ No current evictions or foreclosures  
✓ Bankruptcy acceptable if 2+ years post-discharge  

### Housing History
✓ Rental history (3+ years preferred)  
✓ Evictions: None in last 2 years  
✓ Lease violations: None in last 2 years  

### References
✓ 2-3 personal/professional references  
✓ Previous landlord contact (optional)

## Common Timeline Questions

**Q: Can I move in faster than 2 weeks?**  
A: Sometimes. If lease is ready and background clear, possible in 3-5 days.

**Q: How long does closing actually take?**  
A: 30-45 days after pre-approval. MJ manages timeline with lender.

**Q: What if my credit doesn't improve fast enough?**  
A: MJ extends your lease. No pressure. Credit building takes time.

**Q: Can I close before my lease ends?**  
A: Yes. If approved early, close early with no penalty.

**Q: How long is the home inspection?**  
A: Typically 2-3 hours. Can schedule in month 11 of 12-month path.

## What Delays Timelines?

### Common Delays (Avoidable)
- ❌ Missing documents (provide upfront)
- ❌ Job changes (notify MJ immediately)
- ❌ Missed rent payments (affects approval)

### Uncommon Delays (Beyond Control)
- ✓ Market conditions (lender appraisal issues)
- ✓ Title issues (property history problems)

**Most timelines stay on track. 94% close on schedule.**
