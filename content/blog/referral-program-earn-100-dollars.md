# Earn $100 for Every Friend You Refer: M.J. Newell Homes Referral Program

At M.J. Newell Homes, we believe that homeownership should be accessible to everyone. That's why we've created a simple, rewarding referral program that benefits both you and the people you care about.

## How Our Referral Program Works

Our referral program is straightforward and designed to reward you for helping friends and family achieve their dream of owning a home.

### Step 1: Refer Someone You Know

Do you know someone who is looking to buy a home? Perhaps a friend, family member, coworker, or neighbor who has been dreaming of homeownership? Share M.J. Newell Homes with them.

### Step 2: They Enter Into a Contract

When the person you referred contacts us and enters into a contract with M.J. Newell Homes for one of our new construction homes, you become eligible for the referral reward.

### Step 3: You Earn $100

Once the contract is signed and processed, you'll receive **$100** as a thank you for helping someone achieve their dream of homeownership.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much do I earn per referral?

You earn **$100** for each person you refer who enters into a contract with M.J. Newell Homes.

### When do I receive my referral reward?

You'll receive your $100 reward once the person you referred has signed a contract with M.J. Newell Homes and the contract has been processed.

### Is there a limit to how many people I can refer?

No, there's no limit! You can refer as many people as you'd like. Each successful referral earns you $100.

### What if someone I referred doesn't enter into a contract?

The referral reward is only paid when someone enters into a contract with M.J. Newell Homes. If they don't proceed with a contract, no reward is paid.

### Can I refer family members?

Yes! You can refer family members, friends, coworkers, neighbors, or anyone else who is interested in buying a home.

### Do I need to be a current customer to make referrals?

No, you don't need to be a current customer. Anyone can participate in our referral program.
