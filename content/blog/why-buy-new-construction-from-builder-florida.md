# Why Buy New Construction from a Builder in Florida?

Renting builds no equity. Resale homes may need costly updates. **Buying new construction from a builder** gives you modern design, warranties, and predictable costs.

## Benefits of buying new from M.J. Newell Homes

- **New systems** — HVAC, roof, plumbing with full warranty period ahead of you  
- **Energy-efficient features** — impact windows, insulation, modern appliances  
- **Standard features included** — stone counters, block construction, curated finishes  
- **Fixed purchase price** — agreed upfront on builder contracts  
- **Community choice** — LaBelle or Lehigh Acres locations  

## New home vs renting

| Renting | Buying new |
|--------|------------|
| No equity | Build equity |
| Rent increases | Fixed mortgage potential |
| Limited control | Your home, your rules |
