# How to Apply for MJ Newell Homes Rent-to-Own

Ready to own? The application is simple.

## Step 1: Online Application (5 Minutes)

Visit **mjnewellhomes.com/apply**

Answer these questions:
- Full name, age, phone
- Current city/state
- Employment (job title, employer, salary)
- Credit score estimate (optional)
- Target home price range
- Preferred lease length (12/24/36 months)

**Submit.** Confirmation email sent immediately.

---

## Step 2: Phone Interview (20 Minutes)

Within 24 hours, an MJ advisor calls you:
- Verify employment history
- Discuss credit situation (no judgment)
- Review rent-to-own options
- Answer your questions
- Confirm next steps

**What they're listening for:**
- Income stability
- Willingness to commit to timeline
- Realistic expectations

---

## Step 3: Document Submission (48 Hours)

Gather (upload to MJ portal):
- ✓ Recent pay stubs (2-3)
- ✓ Government ID
- ✓ Bank statements (last 2 months)
- ✓ References (2-3 personal/professional)
- ✓ Employment verification letter (optional)

---

## Step 4: Background Check & Approval (3-7 Days)

MJ pulls:
- Credit report (all three bureaus)
- Employment verification
- Rental history
- Court records (evictions, bankruptcy)

**Decision**: Approval email within 5-7 days

---

## Step 5: Lease Signing & Move-In (1-2 Weeks)

- Lease agreement emailed for signature
- MJ coordinates move-in date
- First rent payment processed
- **Keys handed over**

---

## Documents Needed

### Must-Have
- Government-issued ID (driver's license, passport)
- Most recent 2-3 pay stubs
- Last 2 months bank statements
- Written employment verification letter (ask HR)

### Nice-to-Have
- Offer letter from employer
- Letters of recommendation
- Proof of savings/assets
- Explanation letter (if credit issues)

---

## FAQs: The Application

**Q: Can I apply online only?**  
A: Yes. Phone interview is for clarity, not required.

**Q: What if I'm between jobs?**  
A: If offer letter + start date confirmed, OK.

**Q: Will I be denied?**  
A: 90% approval rate. Most people don't get denied.

**Q: What disqualifies me?**  
- Active eviction proceeding
- Current violent crime conviction
- Fraud/forgery on application

**Q: How long does approval take?**  
A: 3-7 days (5 days average).

**Q: Can I apply if I'm self-employed?**  
A: Yes. Provide 2 years tax returns.

**Q: Is there an application fee?**  
A: No. Free to apply.

---

## What Happens After Approval?

1. **Lease reviewed** (you ask questions)
2. **Lease signed electronically**
3. **Move-in date scheduled** (within 1-2 weeks)
4. **First rent payment** due 30 days from move-in
5. **Keys transferred** at lease signing
