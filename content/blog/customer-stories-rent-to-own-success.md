## Story 1: Sarah — $0 to Homeowner (Bad Credit)

**Background:**
- Age: 31, single mom of two
- Situation: Credit score 520 (medical debt), $0 saved, renting apartment
- Challenge: Impossible to get mortgage with bad credit

**The MJ Path:**
- Lease term: 24 months
- Home: 3-bed in Lehigh Acres ($180k)
- Monthly rent: $1,100 (15% credit = $165/month)

**Month-by-Month Progress:**
- **Month 1**: Moved in with kids, hope restored
- **Months 2-12**: First time paying utilities on time, credit slowly improving
- **Month 12**: Credit score improved to 580 (60-point jump)
- **Month 18**: Credit 620, started repairing old medical debt
- **Month 24**: Credit 680, $3,960 saved in rent credits

**The Closing:**
- Pre-approved for $180k mortgage at 5.8% rate
- Closing costs covered by rent credits
- Down payment: $3,960
- Keys transferred, Sarah became owner

**Today:**
- Own 3-bed home in Lehigh Acres
- Credit now 720 (refinancing at 5.2%)
- Stability for her two kids
- Building generational wealth

**Sarah's Quote:**
> "Three years ago, I never thought I'd own a home. MJ believed in me when nobody else would. Now my kids have roots. That's everything."
